Lesson 4 · Position Sizing

Forecast to position: how a number becomes contracts

Why this lesson? This is where an abstract forecast turns into a concrete "buy 14 contracts." When you trade live, this is the number the system will try to actually hold at your broker. Getting the sizing right is the difference between a system that risks what you intend and one that blows up.

The one formula

The positionSize stage answers: given this forecast, how big a bet?

subsystem position = (combined forecast ÷ 10) × average position

  • forecast ÷ 10 — a forecast of 10 is "average," so it means "hold the average-size bet." A forecast of 20 means "double it"; −5 means "half, and short."
  • average position — how many contracts equal an average-conviction bet, set by volatility targeting (below).

Volatility targeting sets the "average position"

Carver sizes every bet in risk units, not dollars. Your ch.15 config:

capital                = 250,000
percentage_vol_target  = 20%          # willing to risk 20% annualised
annual cash vol target = 50,000       # = 20% of 250k
daily cash vol target  = 3,125        # = 50,000 / 16 (sqrt of ~256 days)

The "average position" is then: how many contracts of EUROSTX have a daily risk of ~3,125? The answer for EUROSTX is about 9.8 contracts. That's the bet size at forecast = 10. A riskier (more volatile) instrument would need fewer contracts to reach the same risk — that's the whole point.

Why this matters for going live Volatility targeting is what makes each instrument contribute roughly equal risk. Live, if one instrument suddenly gets a huge position, your first question is: did its volatility drop (bigger average position) or its forecast spike? This formula tells you exactly where to look.

Putting it together (EUROSTX)

combined forecast   14.849
        ÷ 10        = 1.4849       # 1.48x an average bet
average position    × 9.813
                    -------------
subsystem position  = 14.57 contracts

"Subsystem" means "if EUROSTX were the only thing you traded." In Lesson 5 we shrink this down to its share of the full portfolio.

See it yourself (3 min)

# _learning_sandbox/course/lessons/trace_position.py
from systems.provided.futures_chapter15.basesystem import futures_system
s = futures_system()
I = "EUROSTX"

combined = s.combForecast.get_combined_forecast(I)
avg_pos  = s.positionSize.get_average_position_at_subsystem_level(I)
subpos   = s.positionSize.get_subsystem_position(I)

print("capital / vol target:", s.positionSize.get_notional_trading_capital(),
      "/", s.positionSize.get_percentage_vol_target(), "%")
print("combined forecast :", round(float(combined.iloc[-1]), 3))
print("average position  :", round(float(avg_pos.iloc[-1]), 3), " (bet at forecast=10)")
print("subsystem position:", round(float(subpos.iloc[-1]), 3),
      " (= forecast/10 x avg)")
