Lesson 5 · Portfolio

The portfolio: one instrument's share of the whole

Why this lesson? A subsystem position pretends EUROSTX is your entire account. Reality: it's one of six instruments sharing your capital. The portfolio stage produces the final notional position — the actual number of contracts the live system will hold. This is the last stop before P&L.

The formula (mirror image of Lesson 3)

notional position = subsystem position × instrument weight × IDM

Notice how this rhymes with combining forecasts: back then we had forecast weights and the FDM; here we have instrument weights and the IDM. Same diversification logic, one level up.

1 · Instrument weights — splitting capital across markets

Your six instruments' weights (they sum to 1):

CORN     0.233     EUROSTX  0.200
MXP      0.233     SOFR     0.117
US10     0.117     V2X      0.098
                             ----
                             1.00

EUROSTX gets 20% of the risk budget. So its position is cut to roughly a fifth.

2 · IDM — the instrument diversification multiplier

Just like the FDM boosted a diversified forecast, the IDM boosts a diversified portfolio. Because the six markets don't move together, holding all six is less risky than holding one — so you can safely scale every position up to use your full risk budget. For your system the IDM is 1.89 (nearly doubling positions). The more diversified your instruments, the higher the IDM.

Why this matters for going live The IDM is powerful — 1.89 means you're holding almost twice the contracts a naive split would suggest. That's mathematically justified only if your instruments really are diversified. Add six correlated instruments and a high IDM becomes dangerous leverage. When you customise your universe later, the IDM is a number to watch.

The full chain, reconciled

Every number in this course so far, for EUROSTX, in one place:

raw ewmac8_32       4.073
  x scalar 5.3  ->  21.6  -> capped 20      (Lesson 2)
... blended w/ other rules, x FDM 1.31  ->  combined 14.849   (Lesson 3)
  / 10 x avg position 9.813             ->  subsystem  14.57   (Lesson 4)
  x instr weight 0.20 x IDM 1.89        ->  notional    5.519  (Lesson 5)

Hold ~5.5 EUROSTX contracts, long. That is the whole pipeline, end to end, in numbers you can now derive yourself.

See it yourself (3 min)

# _learning_sandbox/course/lessons/trace_portfolio.py
from systems.provided.futures_chapter15.basesystem import futures_system
s = futures_system()
I = "EUROSTX"

subpos   = s.positionSize.get_subsystem_position(I)
weights  = s.portfolio.get_instrument_weights()
idm      = s.portfolio.get_instrument_diversification_multiplier()
notional = s.portfolio.get_notional_position(I)

print("subsystem position:", round(float(subpos.iloc[-1]), 3))
print("instrument weight :", round(float(weights.iloc[-1][I]), 3))
print("IDM               :", round(float(idm.iloc[-1]), 3))
print("notional position :", round(float(notional.iloc[-1]), 3),
      " (= subsystem x weight x IDM)")
